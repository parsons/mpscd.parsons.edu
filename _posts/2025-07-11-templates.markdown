I focused on enhancing Apple Notes by introducing a much-needed Templates feature. While Apple Notes is widely used for its clean interface and seamless integration across Apple devices, many users—especially power users—turn to competitor note-taking apps for more advanced capabilities.

Through user research and testing, I discovered a common gap: users frequently create similar types of notes (like meeting summaries, class outlines, or daily journals) but have no way to streamline that process within Apple Notes. This results in repetitive manual formatting and a lack of consistency.

Templates feature allows users to create, save, and reuse structured note formats. Accessible from the toolbar, users can choose from default templates or build their own with headings, checklists, and formatting presets. This addition maintains Apple Notes' minimalist design philosophy while offering more efficiency and personalization for everyday workflows.

By addressing the needs of users who crave speed, structure, and consistency, the Templates feature elevates Apple Notes to better serve both casual and advanced users—without sacrificing its simplicity.
