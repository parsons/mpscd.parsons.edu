neuk is a platform for booking private, premium watch-party rooms: small, cinema-grade spaces designed for groups to watch content together. Positioned between home streaming and traditional movie theaters, neuk creates a new category of social entertainment centered around shared viewing.

Users browse nearby rooms, reserve by the hour, and arrive to a private, pre-configured cinema space designed for small groups. Upon entry, guests scan a QR code to check in, and their phones become remotes to control playback, volume, and browsing. Content is accessed through a room-based streaming interface, allowing groups to watch together without logging into personal accounts.

The experience prioritizes social connection over content itself, transforming passive watching into a shared, intentional activity. By removing friction around hosting, setup, and coordination, neuk makes group viewing effortless and repeatable.

Beyond the core experience, neuk introduces a scalable system layer: hourly room pricing, themed spaces, food and beverage upsells, and extensions. The platform positions shared watching as a bookable outing, similar to bowling, karaoke, or escape rooms.

neuk explores how physical environments and digital interfaces can combine to create a new social behavior, turning everyday streaming into a designed, premium, and social experience.
