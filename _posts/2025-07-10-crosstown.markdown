Crosstown is a social discovery app designed to help New York City residents—especially newcomers—build connections while exploring the city. Moving to NYC can be overwhelming, and finding like-minded people to share experiences with isn’t always easy. Crosstown bridges that gap by matching users based on shared interests, favorite activities, and availability, making it effortless to meet new friends and experience the city together.

Whether you're looking for a food tour in Queens, a buddy to check out an art exhibit in Chelsea, or a casual park hangout in Brooklyn, Crosstown helps you find people who want to do the same. The app curates personalized activity suggestions and group outings, ensuring that every meetup is organic and engaging. With flexible matching options—ranging from one-on-one connections to larger group settings—Crosstown adapts to different social preferences and comfort levels.

More than just an event-finding platform, Crosstown is about fostering community. It’s a way for longtime New Yorkers to share their favorite hidden spots with newcomers and for recent arrivals to immerse themselves in the city's culture. By facilitating meaningful interactions, the app transforms the vastness of NYC into something more intimate, welcoming, and easy to navigate.
