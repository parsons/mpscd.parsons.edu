Habit Rabbit is a habit-tracking app inspired by the classic fable The Tortoise and the Hare. But this time, the story continues: after losing the race, the rabbit decides to try again—not with speed alone, but with consistency, commitment, and daily effort.

In Habit Rabbit, users complete daily tasks to earn carrots, which represent progress and can be redeemed for real-life rewards like coffee vouchers and discount coupons. To stay motivated and accountable, users can join photo proof challenges with friends, sharing snapshots of their habit journey while cheering each other on.

The app encourages not just tracking habits, but sticking with them—through small daily check-ins, visual progress, and a warm, supportive community. A cozy, bunny-themed interface brings lightness and fun to the process, reminding users that everyone has their own pace, but showing up every day is what counts.

Whether you’re building new routines or hopping back in like our determined rabbit, Habit Rabbit turns everyday habits into a journey worth celebrating.
