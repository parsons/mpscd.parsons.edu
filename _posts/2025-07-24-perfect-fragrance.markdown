Perfect Fragrance is an online fragrance shopping platform designed to help users discover their ideal perfumes. It provides detailed information on each fragrance, conveys scents through visual and auditory elements, and offers personalized recommendations.

As the fragrance industry continues to grow, one of the biggest challenges of online fragrance shopping remains the inability to smell scents firsthand. Most existing platforms simply list fragrance notes without capturing the story, emotion, or sensory experience behind each scent, making it difficult for buyers to find one that truly resonates with them.

To bridge this gap, Perfect Fragrance introduces a multi-sensory approach, using visual and auditory elements to bring scents to life. Additionally, it features a quick questionnaire that tailors fragrance recommendations based on users' personal preferences, creating a more intuitive and immersive shopping experience.
