Crafted Plates is a modular meal-planning system designed for real life. It meets users where they are, whether that’s with a stocked pantry or limited ingredients, and helps them plan meals with what they already have. By combining pantry awareness, AI-based recipe suggestions, and a guided cooking experience, Crafted Plates reduces the daily friction of “what should I cook today?”

The system begins by understanding available ingredients, offering meal cards that are generated contextually. Whether it’s ingredients about to expire, personal dietary preferences, or limited prep time, suggestions are adapted accordingly.

Each part of the experience is designed to feel like a quiet, supportive companion in the kitchen. From warm, swipe-able meal cards to a clear, timer-integrated cook mode, every touchpoint has been optimized for low cognitive load, especially for users navigating fatigue or busy schedules.

Crafted Plates isn’t just about planning meals, it’s about making planning itself feel intuitive, forgiving, and even enjoyable.

What makes it stand apart is its sensitivity to real-world constraints and its belief that even the smallest nudge, like showing one good recipe instead of many, can bring calm to the everyday chaos of home cooking.
