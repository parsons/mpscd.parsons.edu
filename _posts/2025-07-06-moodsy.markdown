Spending too much time browsing on Netflix, and still don’t know what to watch?

Let Moodsy match your mood and find your perfect Netflix pick—fast and fun!

After researching the behavior and pain points of Netflix users, I found a common problem: people often spend too much time scrolling through content, unsure of what to watch. With endless options and no clear direction, viewers feel overwhelmed—<br>especially when they just want something that matches their current mood.

This led to the idea of Moodsy—an AI-powered mood matcher that helps users pick content based on how they feel. Whether you’re happy, sad, drained, or just want something exciting, Moodsy tunes into your emotions and curates a personalized watchlist in seconds.

We explored several solutions—from genre filters to friend recommendations and even content based on current watchlists—but nothing felt as engaging or relevant as a mood-based approach. Moodsy stood out as the most intuitive, fun, and emotionally connected option.

On the design side, I was inspired by how emotions are often associated with colors. This led to the creation of a playful, color-driven UI using a mood wheel—making the experience both visual and interactive.

Designing with AI in mind was also a key part of this project. With AI becoming an essential trend, I focused on building a feature that learns from user feedback. By allowing users to rate or adjust recommendations, Moodsy improves over time—generating even more accurate and personalized watchlists.
