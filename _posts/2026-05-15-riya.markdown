When I received this topic, I realized I hadn't seen a butterfly in ages. It had become a distant memory. This made me want to explore the connection between butterflies and remembrance, and the way we search for meaning within our memories.

The overall design is symmetric, taking direct inspiration from the natural symmetry of a butterfly's wings. To evoke the feeling of trying to recall something, the images are kept intentionally hazy and only reveal themselves on hover, much like how a memory sharpens the moment you focus on it.

I also wanted to capture something of the butterfly's own perspective. As a playful interaction, I added a Butterfly Vision mode that shifts the color palette to simulate how butterflies perceive the world, through ultraviolet light. Throughout the design, I incorporated butterfly-inspired iconography and wove in the Are.na logo as a subtle visual nod. Finally, the dialog backgrounds are deliberately blurred, meant to feel as thin and delicate as a butterfly's wing.
