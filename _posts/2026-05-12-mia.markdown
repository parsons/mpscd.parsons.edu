Nook was designed to make it easier for people to find genuine connections and experiences in the city. It’s more than just an event-finding app, it’s a space for discovery, shared moments, and new friendships. Having moved between cities and countries, I’ve often felt how difficult it can be to build a sense of belonging, especially in a fast-paced place like New York. Through Nook, I wanted to create something that helps users find not only fun things to do, but also meaningful ways to feel part of a community wherever they are.
