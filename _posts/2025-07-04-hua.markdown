Hua is a flower-booking platform designed for a seamless and personalized shopping experience. Users can browse seasonal collections, explore flowers by color, and easily place orders with a smooth checkout process.

Beyond traditional flower ordering, Hua offers the Create Your Own Bouquet feature, allowing customers to design unique floral arrangements with real-time previews. Additionally, the Monthly Flower initiative donates a portion of proceeds from featured flowers to charitable causes, turning every purchase into an opportunity to give back.

By combining thoughtful design with meaningful interactions, hua transforms the way people experience flowers—making the process effortless, creative, and socially impactful.
